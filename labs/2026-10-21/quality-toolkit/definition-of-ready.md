# Definition of Ready

A backlog item is ready for sprint planning when:

- [ ] The user or business outcome is clear.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are observable and testable.
- [ ] Dependencies and external decisions are identified.
- [ ] Data, privacy, accessibility, and security impacts are considered.
- [ ] The team can estimate the work and identify a small vertical slice.
- [ ] Open questions have an owner and a target resolution date.

Do not use this checklist as a substitute for team discussion.
