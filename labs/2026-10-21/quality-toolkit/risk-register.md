# Lightweight Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acceptance criteria are ambiguous | Medium | High | Add examples and review with a stakeholder | Product owner | Rework during review |
| A dependency arrives late | Medium | Medium | Identify fallback scope and confirm dates early | Team | Missed dependency checkpoint |
| Regression escapes testing | Low/Medium | High | Add a focused regression test and smoke checklist | Developer | Repeated defect |

Review risks during planning and when assumptions change.
