# Architecture Decision Record

## Title
Short, specific decision name.

## Status
Proposed / Accepted / Superseded.

## Context
What constraint or problem requires a decision?

## Decision
State the choice and important boundaries.

## Alternatives considered
List realistic alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences
Record benefits, trade-offs, migration cost, and follow-up work.

Keep records concise and link related issues instead of copying their full content.
