# Release Readiness Checklist

- [ ] Required tests and reviews passed.
- [ ] Data migrations and compatibility reviewed.
- [ ] Monitoring and alert ownership clear.
- [ ] User-facing changes and limitations documented.
- [ ] Rollback or forward-fix criteria agreed.
- [ ] Release owner available during rollout.
- [ ] Post-release verification steps written down.
- [ ] Exceptions include rationale, approver, and review date.

Adapt this checklist to risk and deployment style. Do not mark a gate complete without evidence.
