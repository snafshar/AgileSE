# Sprint Retrospective

Keep the discussion focused on the system of work, not blame.

## What helped us deliver?
List specific behaviors, tools, decisions, or collaboration practices worth repeating.

## What caused friction?
Describe observable events and their impact.

## What did we learn?
Capture assumptions that proved false and evidence that changed the team's understanding.
