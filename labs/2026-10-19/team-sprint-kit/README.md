# Team Sprint Kit

Reusable lightweight templates for small software teams. The kit is designed to make decisions, acceptance criteria, testing, risks, and learning explicit without requiring a specific project-management platform.

## Included templates

- `product-brief.md`: problem, users, scope, outcomes, constraints.
- `sprint-plan.md`: sprint goal, backlog, capacity, dependencies.
- `definition-of-done.md`: shared completion criteria.
- `test-strategy.md`: test levels and release checks.
- `risk-register.csv`: starter risk log.
- `retrospective.md`: guided review of the sprint.
- `issue-template.md`: a concise, testable engineering issue.

Copy the templates into a project and tailor them with the team. They are prompts for discussion, not a replacement for understanding the product.
